Privacy Policy: Hollowguard

Effective date: 2026-11-21

Hollowguard ("the game") is made by VortexGrid Interactive ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. Questions: hollowguard3@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You play as a guest: no account is needed and no email, phone number or location is ever asked for. A name you type is only a display name on your device. Signing in with Google Play Games is optional.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, heroes, gold, gems, settings) is saved on your device. If you are not signed in, it stays there: deleting the game deletes it. We do not upload it to any server of ours. (If Android backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android may include it in your own Google backup.)
  • The game is fully playable offline.
  • Optional ads are shown by Google AdMob, and optional purchases are handled by Google Play. These Google services process some device data as described below.
  • The game can send you reminders (for example "your free chest is ready"). They are created on your phone, need no account and can be turned off at any time.
  • You can delete everything the game stores at any time: Settings > Privacy > Delete my data.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your game progress, your privacy choice, your age group (from the birth year screen; the year itself is used only to decide which privacy rules apply) and simple gameplay statistics (for example which levels were won) in the game's private storage on your device. We do not receive this information. If Android's backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may copy the game's saved data into your private Google backup so it can be restored on a new phone; you control this in your Android settings. The data on the device is removed when you use "Delete my data" or uninstall the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and may show ads, for example an optional video you choose to watch for a reward. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about the ad interaction, as described in Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

  • Personalised ads are shown only if you choose "Accept all" on the privacy screen.
  • If you choose "Essential only", or if you are under 13, only non-personalised ads are shown and no analytics consent is given.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is loaded. You can reopen it at any time in Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy choices.
  • You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy, and reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Game analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

If you choose "Accept all" (and you are 13 or older), the game sends anonymous gameplay events to Google Analytics for

Firebase so we can find levels that are too hard, screens that confuse players and errors to fix. Examples: which

level you started, won or lost and at which wave, which screen you were on when you closed the game, gold and gems

earned and spent, and your app version, language, device model and country. These events are linked to a random app

instance id, not to your name, account or advertising ID (advertising-ID collection is turned off). Nothing is sent if

you choose "Essential only" or are under 13. You can switch it off at any time in Settings > Privacy; "Delete my

data" also resets the analytics id. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from

apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google Play Games (optional)

Nothing is sent to Google Play Games unless you choose to sign in; players under 13 cannot use it. If you sign in:

  • Cloud save: your game progress is stored in your own Google Play Games account so you can continue on another phone. Your privacy choice, age group and device settings stay on your device.
  • World rankings: your scores, with your Play Games name and profile, are visible to other players on the rankings. You choose that name and profile's visibility in the Play Games app, not in this game. The rankings show other players' Play Games names in the same way.

Google processes this data under its policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. We have no server of our own and do not receive your email address.

To delete: Settings > Privacy > Delete my data also deletes your cloud save. To remove your ranking entries, delete this game's data from your profile in the Google Play Games app.

Reminders (notifications)

If you turn them on, the game schedules a few reminders on your phone (for example a free chest is ready, your Treasury is full, or a daily reward is waiting). They are created and shown by your phone itself: nothing about them is sent to us or to any server. They are never shown between 22:00 and 08:00. Android 13 and newer asks your permission first, after you have played a few levels. Turn them off in Settings > Reminders or in your Android notification settings.

Rating and sharing (optional)

  • Rate the game: after a great win, the game may show Google's in-app review card (at most a few times). Google Play handles the card and any review you write, under Google's privacy policy. We only keep, on your device, how many times we asked.
  • Share: if you tap Share on a result screen, the game gives a picture of your result, a short message and the store link to Android's share sheet. You choose the app and the person; we do not receive anything.

Purchases (Google Play)

Optional in-app purchases (for example "Remove Ads" or gem packs) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The game keeps a record of which items were bought so it can deliver and restore them. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and is not directed to children under 13 as its main audience, but we protect younger players. On first launch it asks for a birth year using a neutral screen. Players under 13 get a kid-safe mode: no personalised ads and no analytics (nothing is sent to Firebase). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your ad and analytics choice: Settings > Privacy.
  • Delete all game data: Settings > Privacy > Delete my data (this also resets your progress).
  • Contact us about privacy, including requests under GDPR or CCPA, at hollowguard3@gmail.com (we reply within 30 days). Because we hold no data about you on our own servers, requests about ad data are best sent to Google (https://policies.google.com/privacy), and we will help you with them.

Security

Your progress stays in the game's private app storage, which other apps cannot read. Communication with Google's ad and billing services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Data we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. We do not sell personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Questions about this policy: hollowguard3@gmail.com.
